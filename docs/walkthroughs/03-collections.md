# 03 · Collections

**Target length:** 2:30
**Audience:** anyone with content management rights
**Pages:** `/admin/collections`, `/admin/catalog`, the viewer site
**Prerequisites:** at least four Ready videos (sample content is enough).

## Scene 1 — What a collection is (0:00–0:20)

**STAGE:** `/admin/collections`. Panel "Collections", subtitle "Group videos,
seasons, and series for the viewer catalog." Table columns Title, Visible,
Position. Sample collections listed with "Visible" pills.

**VO:** A collection is a hand-picked group of videos with its own page on
your site. Think of a shelf: "Staff picks", "Start here", "Behind the
scenes". Viewers browse it as one page, and you can pin it as a row on your
homepage.

## Scene 2 — Create one (0:20–0:55)

**STAGE:** Click **New Collection**. Sheet "New Collection" with Title,
Description, "Cover image" ("JPG, PNG or WebP. Optional."). Type "Start Here"
and a one-line description. Drop a cover image. Click **Save**. The new row
appears in the table.

**VO:** New Collection. Give it a title and a short description. The cover
image is optional; if you skip it the collection uses artwork from its first
video. Save.

## Scene 3 — Add items (0:55–1:35)

**STAGE:** Click the collection title. Detail panel shows the title as heading,
description as subtitle, and "No items yet". Click **Add Items**. Sheet "Add
Items", subtitle "Pick videos, seasons, or series to include." with tabs
Videos, Seasons, Series. Tick three videos, switch to Series and tick one
sample series; the footer reads "4 selected". Click **Add Selected**. The
items list fills in. Use the ↑ ↓
buttons to move one item to the top.

**VO:** Open it and click Add Items. You can add single videos, whole
seasons, or an entire series. Order matters: the order here is the order
viewers see, so use the arrows to arrange it.

## Scene 4 — Visibility (1:35–1:55)

**STAGE:** Click **← Back**. In the table click the "Visible" pill on the new
collection. It flips to "Hidden". Click it again back to "Visible".

**VO:** The Visible toggle hides a collection from your site without deleting
it. Handy while you're still filling one in.

## Scene 5 — Put it on the homepage (1:55–2:20)

**STAGE:** Sidebar → **Catalog**. Click **New Row**. Title "Start Here",
"Source Type" → Collection, "Collection" → Start Here. Save. Click **View
site**; the new row is on the homepage. Click the row's "See all" link to land
on the collection page.

**VO:** To feature it, go to Catalog, add a row, choose Collection as the
source and pick yours. It shows up on the homepage right away, with a link
through to the full collection page.

## Scene 6 — Close (2:20–2:30)

**STAGE:** Collection page on the viewer site. Hold.

**VO:** Next up: Series and seasons, for content that should be watched in
order.

## Rough edges

- Delete asks "Are you sure?" and soft-deletes. Skip it.
- Row titles do not have to match the collection title; the script uses the
  same name for clarity.
