# 05 · Homepage rows

**Target length:** 3:30
**Audience:** anyone with content management rights
**Pages:** `/admin/catalog`, the viewer site
**Prerequisites:** sample content present (gives you rows to look at), one
collection and one tag of your own.

## Scene 1 — Catalog is your homepage (0:00–0:25)

**STAGE:** `/admin/catalog`. Header "Catalog". The "Hero Carousel" section is
at the top (collapse it with its chevron so the rows table fills the frame).
Rows table columns: Title, Source, Card variant, Visible, Order. Sample rows:
"Staff Picks" (Curated), "Getting Started with Marquee" (Collection), "Sample
Series: Behind the Lens" (Collection), "Recently Added" (Recent).

**VO:** Catalog is the homepage builder. The page your viewers land on is a
hero banner followed by a stack of rows, and this table is that stack, top to
bottom. Each row has a source, which decides what fills it, and a card
variant, which decides how it looks.

## Scene 2 — Sources (0:25–1:15)

**STAGE:** Click **New Row**. Modal "New Row": Title, "Source Type" select.
Open the select and pause on the options: Curated, Collection, Tag, Recent,
Popular, Continue Watching, "Series with new seasons (auto-populated)", and
the preset row types. Choose **Tag**; a "Tag" select appears ("Select a
tag..."). Pick your tag. Title the row "Trailers". Save. It appears at the
bottom of the table.

**VO:** Curated means you pick each video by hand. Collection and Tag fill
themselves from a collection or tag you already have, so new videos show up
without touching the row. Recent and Popular are automatic. Continue Watching
is per viewer and only appears once they've started something. Let's make a
tag row.

## Scene 3 — Curated items (1:15–1:45)

**STAGE:** On "Staff Picks" (Curated) click **Items**. The row items view
lists the current videos. Open the "Add Videos" picker; it has two tabs, "From
Content" and "Upload new". Add one video from Content, remove another from the
row, use the arrows to reorder. Return to the table.

**VO:** For a curated row, Items is where you pick and order videos. Use it
for anything editorial: a weekly feature, a themed set, a "start here" strip.

## Scene 4 — Card variant, visibility, order (1:45–2:35)

**STAGE:** On the "Trailers" row change the "Card variant" select (options
depend on source; show poster versus landscape). Click the "Visible" pill; it
turns "Hidden". Click again. Click ↑ twice to move the row up. Click
**Preview** on it; a preview strip of the row's videos renders under the
table.

**VO:** Card variant switches between poster art and landscape cards for
that row. Visible hides a row without losing it. The arrows set the order.
Preview shows what the row will contain before you go look at the site. And
Edit lets you rename a row or change its source later.

## Scene 5 — On the site (2:35–3:10)

**STAGE:** **View site**. Scroll the homepage: hero, then the rows in the
order just set, with "Trailers" near the top and its cards in the chosen
variant. Use the row's scroll arrows once.

**VO:** Here's the result. Rows appear in the order you set, with the cards
you chose. Changes apply live: viewers with the page open see the new layout
without reloading.

## Scene 6 — Close (3:10–3:30)

**STAGE:** Back on `/admin/catalog`. Expand the "Hero Carousel" section so
its slide list is visible. Hold.

**VO:** The other half of the homepage is the hero banner at the top. That's
the next video.

## Rough edges

- The Appearance page's preset chooser can overwrite every row here. It is
  covered, and skipped, in script 07.
- "Welcome text block" is a text-only row type; leave it out of this script.
- Delete asks "Are you sure?". Do not demo it.
