# 01 · Welcome to your dashboard

**Target length:** 2:30
**Audience:** owner or admin on their first login
**Pages:** `/admin`, the viewer site
**Prerequisites:** fresh trial org, sample content present, guided tour not yet
completed by this operator, setup wizard already finished.

## Scene 1 — Landing (0:00–0:20)

**STAGE:** `/admin`. The "Free trial — **N** days left." banner and the sample
content banner sit at the top. The Shepherd tour has auto-opened its centered
"Welcome to <org name>!" card. Leave it open for a beat, then click **Next**.

**VO:** This is your Marquee dashboard. The first time you land here a short
tour points out each section of the sidebar. We'll follow it, then look at the
pieces you'll use most.

## Scene 2 — The tour (0:20–0:55)

**STAGE:** Click **Next** through the tour. Each card attaches to a sidebar
link, top to bottom: Dashboard, Content, Collections, Series, Tags, Catalog,
Podcasts, Landing Page, Analytics, Appearance, Members, Webhooks, Settings,
Billing, Audit Log, then "View Your Site". Keep a steady one-second cadence.
Finish on the "You're all set!" card and click **Finish**.

**VO:** Content is where videos live. Collections and Series group them.
Catalog builds your homepage. Appearance controls colors and fonts. Settings
and Billing handle payments, and everything else is what you'd expect. You can
replay this any time from the "Take a tour" link at the top of the dashboard.

## Scene 3 — Setup nudges (0:55–1:20)

**STAGE:** Scroll so the "Setup tasks" cards fill the frame. On a sample
org two cards show: "Connect Stripe to accept payments" and "Create a
subscription plan" (the branding and homepage cards are already satisfied by
the setup wizard and the sample rows). Hover the "Set up payments" link on the
first card. Do not click. Hover the × on one card.

**VO:** Below the header are your setup tasks. Right now that's payments: a
Stripe account and a plan. Each card links straight to the page that finishes
it, and each disappears on its own once the task is done. If you'd rather not
see one, the × hides it for you.

## Scene 4 — KPIs and recent activity (1:20–1:45)

**STAGE:** Scroll to the KPI row: "Active Subscribers", "MRR", "Total Views
(30d)", "Published Videos". Then the two columns "Recent Uploads" (lists the
sample videos with a green "Ready" badge) and "Recent Signups" ("No viewers
yet."). Then "Top Content (30d)" with "No viewing data yet."

**VO:** The numbers update as your service grows. Recent Uploads shows the
last five videos and whether they've finished processing. Recent Signups and
Top Content fill in as viewers arrive. The "Full analytics" link goes deeper.

## Scene 5 — Your site (1:45–2:15)

**STAGE:** Click **View site** in the sidebar footer. A new tab opens on the
viewer homepage: the "Featured" hero with two sample slides, then rows "Staff
Picks", "Getting Started with Marquee", "Sample Series: Behind the Lens" and
"Recently Added". Scroll slowly through the rows. Switch back to the admin tab.

**VO:** "View site" opens what your viewers see. Right now that's sample
content so the page isn't empty. When you're ready, the banner at the top of
the admin has a "Clear sample content" link that removes all of it in one
click.

## Scene 6 — Close (2:15–2:30)

**STAGE:** Admin tab, dashboard, sidebar in frame. Hold.

**VO:** Next: uploading your first video. Then collections, your homepage,
branding, and taking payments. Each one is a few minutes.

## Rough edges

- If the tour does not auto-open, this operator has already completed it.
  Use "Take a tour" and cut the first second.
- The "Published Videos" KPI counts a flag that operator uploads never set.
  Do not zoom on it.
