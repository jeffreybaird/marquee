# 10 · The landing page

**Target length:** 3:00
**Audience:** anyone with content management rights
**Pages:** `/admin/landing`, the viewer site logged out
**Prerequisites:** at least one plan (for the "Plan display" section) and one
collection; a browser profile that is logged out of the viewer site.

## Scene 1 — Who sees it (0:00–0:25)

**STAGE:** Logged-out browser on the viewer site root. It shows the org
landing page (sample content orgs have none yet, so it's minimal). Switch to
the admin: `/admin/landing`. Header "Landing Page", subtitle "Build the page
visitors see before they sign up." Empty state "No landing sections yet. Add
one below." and the "+ Add section…" select.

**VO:** Logged-in viewers get your catalog. Everyone else, someone who just
clicked a link, gets the landing page. This is your pitch, and it's built from
sections you stack in order.

## Scene 2 — Add sections (0:25–1:20)

**STAGE:** Open "+ Add section…": options Hero video, Hero image, Marketing
copy, Content row, Plan display, Header text, FAQ. Choose **Hero image**;
flash "Section added." and the section appears in the list with its type and
a preview line. Add **Marketing copy**, **Content row**, **Plan display** and
**FAQ** the same way. Five items in the list.

**VO:** Pick a section type and it's added to the bottom. Hero video or hero
image for the top. Marketing copy for your paragraph. Content row shows a
real collection from your catalog as a teaser. Plan display pulls in your
subscription plans with prices. FAQ for the questions you keep answering.

## Scene 3 — Edit a section (1:20–2:05)

**STAGE:** Click **Edit** on the Hero image section. The editor opens below:
"Edit Hero image" with Headline, Subheadline, "Image URL", "CTA text", "CTA
link", show/hide toggles for headline, subheadline and "CTA button", "Overlay
opacity". Fill headline "Woodworking, taught properly", CTA text "Start free
trial", CTA link `/subscribe`. Save; flash "Section saved." Edit the Content
row section, pick your collection, set "Max items" 6, save. Edit FAQ, add two
items, save.

**VO:** Each section has its own fields. For the hero: headline, subheadline,
an image, and a call-to-action button that should point at subscribe. Content
row picks a collection and how many cards to show. FAQ items are added one at
a time. Save each section as you go.

## Scene 4 — Order and visibility (2:05–2:30)

**STAGE:** Use ▲ ▼ to move Plan display above FAQ. Click **Hide** on
Marketing copy; it dims and the button reads **Show**. Click Show. Point at
Delete without clicking.

**VO:** Arrows set the order. Hide keeps a section but takes it off the page,
useful for seasonal promos. Delete removes it for good.

## Scene 5 — The result (2:30–2:55)

**STAGE:** Logged-out browser, reload the site root. The landing page renders
hero, copy, content row, plans, FAQ. Scroll it. Click the hero CTA to land on
the subscribe or register flow.

**VO:** Reload as a visitor and there it is: hero, your copy, a taste of the
catalog, your plans, and the FAQ, in the order you set. The button takes them
straight into signup.

## Scene 6 — Close (2:55–3:00)

**STAGE:** Landing page. Hold.

**VO:** Next: managing your viewers.

## Rough edges

- Hero video sections can link an existing video or upload one to Mux via
  the picker sheet; skip it to stay under time.
- Delete asks "Delete this section?".
